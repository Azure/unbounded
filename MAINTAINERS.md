# Maintainers

The Unbounded Kubernetes maintainer team is
[`@Azure/unbounded-dev`](https://github.com/orgs/Azure/teams/unbounded-dev).
The team is the repository-wide owner recorded in
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS) and is responsible for technical
direction, reviews, releases, and project governance.

Individual team membership is managed by the Azure organization and is visible
on GitHub to users with access to the team's public membership. Maintainers who
represent the project in a public foundation application must separately consent
to publication of their name, affiliation, and contact information.

The process for adding, removing, and stepping down as a maintainer is defined
in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
