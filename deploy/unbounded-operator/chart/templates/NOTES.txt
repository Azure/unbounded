unbounded-operator {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Verify the rollout; the operator installs the Unbounded CRDs at startup:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deployment/unbounded-operator

Then create Sites, for example:
  kubectl unbounded site init --name <site> ...

Uninstall removes the operator, its RBAC and ConfigMap. CRDs, Sites and the
components the operator reconciled are left in place.
